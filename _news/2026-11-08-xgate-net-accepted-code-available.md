Our paper, “XGate-Net: Gated Cross-Task Interaction for Efficient Multi-Task Perception in Autonomous Driving,” has been accepted in Signal, Image and Video Processing.

The paper was authored by Sungjun Jang, Minkyoung Kim, Dongjin Jeon, and Taegeun Oh. This work grew out of the undergraduate graduation research course in the Department of Electronic Engineering at Dong Seoul University.

XGate-Net jointly performs semantic segmentation and depth estimation from a single RGB image. The model implementation, training code, and inference code are publicly available through the Code link below.
