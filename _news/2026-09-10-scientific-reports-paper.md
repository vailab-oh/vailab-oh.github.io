Our paper, **“A comparative analysis of deep learning models for disease classification in multi-organ histopathological images,”** has been published in **Scientific Reports**, a Nature Portfolio journal. The journal is indexed in **SCIE** and is ranked in **Q1** according to the Journal Citation Reports (JCR). Scientific Reports currently reports a **2025 Journal Impact Factor of 4.9**.

This study investigates deep learning approaches for disease classification using histopathological whole-slide images from multiple organs, including the heart, lung, liver, and pancreas. CNN- and Vision Transformer-based models were systematically compared, with the results demonstrating strong performance for multi-organ disease classification.

The work was conducted through collaboration between **Dong Seoul University**, the **Medical Device Development Center at K-MEDI hub (Daegu-Gyeongbuk Medical Innovation Foundation)**, and **Pusan National University**. Taegeun Oh served as the **corresponding author** and supervised the research.

This collaboration represents our continued research efforts in **AI-based medical image analysis and intelligent visual perception**, while expanding interdisciplinary cooperation with medical-device and biomedical research institutes.
