Our **H&E- and Nissl-stained histopathological rabbit brain image dataset for photothrombotic lesion classification** has been publicly released on **Zenodo** with a persistent DOI. The dataset was developed in collaboration between **Dong Seoul University** and the **Daegu-Gyeongbuk Medical Innovation Foundation (K-MEDI hub)**.

The dataset contains histopathological image patches obtained from rabbit brain tissues following photothrombotic ischemic stroke induction. It includes three tissue categories—**photothrombotic lesions, necrotic regions, and normal tissue**—across both **H&E and Nissl staining modalities**. A total of **4,350 images** are provided, with 2,175 images for each staining modality.

The dataset supports research on **histopathological image classification, ischemic stroke analysis, deep learning benchmarking, and preclinical brain tissue analysis**. It was used in our related study published in *Biomedical Signal Processing and Control*.

The dataset is openly available through Zenodo under DOI **10.5281/zenodo.21787811**.
