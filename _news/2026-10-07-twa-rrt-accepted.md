The paper “Time-Window-Aware Sampling-Based Path Planning for Time-Constrained Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Missions,” authored by Taegeun Oh, has been accepted in the AIAA Journal of Aerospace Information Systems. The acceptance notification was received on October 7, 2026.

TWA-RRT* plans collision-free UAV paths while accounting for prescribed arrival-time windows at a fixed flight speed. The research code is available through the Code link below.
