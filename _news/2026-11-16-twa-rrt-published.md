The paper “Time-Window-Aware Sampling-Based Path Planning for Time-Constrained Unmanned Aerial Vehicle Missions,” authored by Taegeun Oh, is now published in the AIAA Journal of Aerospace Information Systems.

TWA-RRT* plans collision-free UAV paths while accounting for prescribed arrival-time windows at a fixed flight speed.

Following our earlier acceptance announcement, we are pleased to share the published article and research code through the Paper and Code links below.
